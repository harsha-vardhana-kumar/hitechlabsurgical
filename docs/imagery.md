# Asset provenance

The website uses three original raster images generated with the built-in image
generation tool for this project. They are illustrative compositions, not evidence
of owned facilities, precise product appearance, stock, certifications or brands.
All three were inspected and contain no people, logos or product labels.

| File | Use | Original dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apps/website/public/images/laboratory.webp` | Hero, about and equipment-category illustration | 1536 × 1024 |
| `apps/website/public/images/consumables.webp` | Consumable/collection-category illustration | 1536 × 1024 |
| `apps/website/public/images/reagents.webp` | Reagent-category illustration | 1536 × 1024 |

The originals were encoded as WebP with no visual edits. The exact generation
prompts are in `asset-prompts.json`. Product images remain individually replaceable.
`product-placeholder.svg` is a neutral local fallback for unavailable images.

The text wordmark and H favicon are temporary typographic identifiers, pending the
client's approved brand assets. The Manrope font is distributed under the licence
included at `apps/website/public/fonts/LICENSE-manrope.txt`.
